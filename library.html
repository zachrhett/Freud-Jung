What you will not find hosted here

  • The Standard Edition of the Complete Psychological Works of Sigmund Freud (Strachey).
  • The Collected Works of C. G. Jung (Hull / Princeton / Routledge).
  • The Freud/Jung Letters and the two-volume C. G. Jung Letters.
  • The Red Book / Liber Novus and the Black Books.
  • The forthcoming Princeton Critical Edition of Jung.
  • Unofficial “complete works” PDFs that mix protected translations into one file.

Those belong in a library, a licensed database (PEP-Web), or a purchased edition. See Archives.

How to use a PD text well

  1. Note the translator. Brill is historically important and often rough. Hall’s 1920 lectures are a teaching text, not the Standard Edition.
  2. Compare a paragraph with a later scholarly translation when you cite for research.
  3. Keep the original date in your notes. Freud revised books across decades.
