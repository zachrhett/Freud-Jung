Workbooks

Study tools, not diagnostic instruments

Original exercises for reading Freud and Jung from the inside. Nothing here is scored against a clinical norm. Nothing here is a diagnosis.

If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or, in the United States, call or text 988. These workbooks are educational.

1. Dream worksheet

Write a recent dream in the present tense. Then use both interpretive paths described in Methods.

2. Word-association exercise (after Jung)

Answer with the first word that comes. Do not improve it. In the clinic Jung timed the pause; here, simply notice hesitation.

3. Defense-mechanism reflection

Check any statement that felt true more than once this month. Labels follow the ego-psychological vocabulary that grew from Freud and Anna Freud.

4. Psychological-types sketch (Jung 1921, not MBTI)

Move each slider toward the pole that feels more like your habitual stance. This is a teaching prompt based on Psychological Types, not the Myers–Briggs Type Indicator.
